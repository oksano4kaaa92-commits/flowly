Privacy Policy

Flowly respects your privacy.

Flowly is a browser extension designed to help users stay focused and reduce distractions.

Information We Collect

Flowly does not collect personal information such as names, passwords, payment information, or private messages.

The extension may locally store settings and focus statistics on your device in order to provide core functionality.

Website Permissions

Flowly requires access to certain websites only to block distracting content selected by the user and display focus-related overlays.

Analytics

Flowly currently does not collect analytics or personal usage data.

In the future, anonymous and privacy-friendly analytics may be introduced to improve product performance and features.

Third-Party Services

Payments and subscriptions may be processed by third-party providers such as Lemon Squeezy.
